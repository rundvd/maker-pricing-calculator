This Etsy fee calculator 2026 guide shows what Etsy charges a US seller and how those charges affect one order. Use the $25 item and $5 shipping example to check your own price before listing.

How much does Etsy take from a US sale?

For a standard online order, start with the listing fee, transaction fee, and Etsy Payments processing fee. Add an Offsite Ads fee only for an attributed order. Other charges depend on the services you use or your shop setup.

Listing and renewal fees

Etsy charges $0.20 to publish a listing, even if it never sells. A listing expires after four months, and renewal costs another $0.20. For multiple quantities, Etsy charges $0.20 to renew each additional item sold. A private listing costs $0.20 when purchased. Editing has no listing fee.

Transaction and payment processing fees

The transaction fee is 6.5% of the item price plus any shipping and gift wrap you charge. For US sellers, Etsy does not apply this transaction fee to sales tax. Optional paid personalization adds to the displayed item price and is included in the fee base.

For a US bank account, Etsy Payments processing costs 3% of the gross order plus $0.25 per order. Processing includes shipping and applicable sales tax. This is separate from the 6.5% transaction charge. The flat $0.25 applies per order, not per item.

Offsite Ads fees

An attributed Offsite Ads order adds 15% of the order amount if your shop made less than $10,000 in Etsy sales during the prior 365 days. Shops that reach at least $10,000 pay 12% on attributed orders, with a $100 cap on the Offsite Ads fee for one order. Shipping and gift wrap you charge are part of the order amount used for this fee. For US sellers, sales tax is excluded.

Below $10,000, you can opt out. Once your shop reaches that threshold, participation is required and the 12% rate continues for the shop's lifetime, even if later sales fall. These fees apply only to attributed orders.

Other charges you may see

Etsy may charge a one-time, nonrefundable shop setup fee. The amount varies by location and appears during setup; there is no fixed US rate. A 2.5% conversion fee applies when your listing and Etsy Payments account currencies differ.

Etsy Ads are optional, with spending controlled by your daily budget. Shipping labels, signature service, and insurance cost extra; prices depend on the package and service. Etsy Plus is $10 monthly. Pattern has a 30 day free trial, then costs $15 monthly. An in-person Square sale not synced to Etsy inventory has a $0.20 Square manual fee, plus Square's processing charge.

Select US sellers in Etsy's instant transfer beta pay 1% of the transfer amount, with a $0.25 minimum and $1 maximum. The fee appears before transfer. Sales tax may apply to certain Etsy seller fees. Etsy's Regulatory Operating and deposit fees listed for certain other countries do not apply to a standard US bank account.

Worked example: $25 item plus $5 shipping

Assume one item, no gift wrap, no sales tax, no optional services, and no Offsite Ads attribution. The buyer pays $30 before any tax. The listing costs $0.20, the transaction fee is 6.5% × $30 = $1.95, and US processing is 3% × $30 + $0.25 = $1.15. Total Etsy fees are $3.30. That leaves $26.70 before the actual cost of shipping, materials, labor, and other business expenses.

If the same order came through Offsite Ads, 15% adds $4.50, making total fees $7.80 and leaving $22.20 before your costs. At the 12% rate, the ad fee is $3.60, total fees are $6.90, and $23.10 remains before your costs. The $0.20 listing fee may have been charged earlier, but it still belongs in the item's cost.

FAQ

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes. The 6.5% transaction fee applies to shipping you charge, and US payment processing includes shipping in the order total. Offsite Ads fees also include charged shipping when the order is attributed to those ads.

Does Etsy charge a fee if my item does not sell?

The $0.20 listing fee is charged when you publish, even without a sale. It can be charged again if the listing renews after four months. Transaction and processing fees require a sale.

Can I opt out of Etsy Offsite Ads?

You can opt out while your shop is below $10,000 in Etsy sales over the prior 365 days. Once your shop reaches that threshold, participation is required under Etsy's current policy. The 12% rate then applies to attributed orders.

Is the $25 item example my profit?

No. The amount after Etsy fees still has to cover postage, the item, packaging, labor, failures, and other costs. Subtract those costs before calling the remainder profit.
